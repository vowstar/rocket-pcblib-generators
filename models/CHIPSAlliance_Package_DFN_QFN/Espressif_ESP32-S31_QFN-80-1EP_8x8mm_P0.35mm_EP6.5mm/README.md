# Espressif ESP32-S31 QFN-80

The nominal STEP follows Figure 7-1 of the ESP32-S31 Series Datasheet v0.5.
The body is 8.0 x 8.0 mm and the total height is 0.85 mm.
Leads are 0.17 mm wide, 0.40 mm long and 0.203 mm thick at a 0.35 mm pitch.
The exposed pad is 6.5 x 6.5 mm. The contact plane is Z = 0.

Pin 1 is the top lead on the left side. Numbering runs anticlockwise in top view,
which matches the footprint. The pin 1 dot, exposed-pad corner chamfer and
display colors are simplified. They do not define manufacturing tolerances.
