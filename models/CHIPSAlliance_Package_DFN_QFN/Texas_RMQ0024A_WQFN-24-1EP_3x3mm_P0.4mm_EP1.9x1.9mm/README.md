# Texas Instruments RMQ0024A WQFN-24

The nominal STEP follows TI package outline RMQ0024A, drawing 4221088 revision B.
The body is 3.0 x 3.0 mm with a nominal height of 0.75 mm (0.8 mm maximum).
Each side has five 0.25 mm leads at 0.4 and 0.5 mm offsets. The center lead is
0.4 mm wide and the others are 0.2 mm wide. Corner leads are 0.25 mm squares.
The exposed pad is 1.9 x 1.9 mm. The contact plane is Z = 0.

Pin 1 is the top lead on the left side in top view, which matches the footprint.
The pin 1 dot, corner lead shape, exposed-pad chamfer and display colors are
simplified. They do not define manufacturing tolerances.
