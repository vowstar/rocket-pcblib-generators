# Sunlord WPN252010U

The nominal body is 2.50 × 2.00 mm at the 1.00 mm maximum height.
The two terminals have 0.60 × 2.00 mm bounding dimensions, a 1.30 mm gap,
and a Z=0 contact plane. Edge bevels, terminal thickness and colors are
rendering approximations.

The recommended PCB lands are 0.80 × 2.00 mm with a 1.20 mm inner gap.
The footprint uses a 0.20 mm pad corner radius.

The generator builds the model from dimensions and uses Apache-2.0.
