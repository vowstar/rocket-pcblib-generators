# ALPS Alpine SKRTLBE010

The nominal STEP follows the ALPS Alpine drawing for the 4.5 mm side-push
switch without guide bosses. The seating plane is Z = 0 and the plunger projects
towards −Y. The flange, mounting tabs, electrical terminals and plunger are solid
geometry. The model simplifies stamping details and the underside.

The finish is based on distributor photographs of LCSC C127481 and the
manufacturer photo. It uses a pale ivory plunger, a dark molded body and
silver-colored terminals and metal shield.
