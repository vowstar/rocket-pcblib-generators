# Hanrun HR911105A

The nominal STEP follows the Hanrun HR911105A datasheet, revision A/2, page 2.
The shielded body is 16.04 mm wide, 21.30 mm deep and 13.50 mm high.
Through-board leads extend 3.05 mm below the seating plane at Z = 0.
The jack opens towards -Y with the latch slot down, which matches the footprint.

The green LED is on the left and the yellow LED on the right in front view.
The shield is a hollow sheet around a black insert. Contact wires, magnetics,
shield springs and lead forms are simplified display geometry.
